Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no credits, no watermark and no account. Make as many codes as you like.

Will my QR code stop working one day?

No. These are static codes: the information is stored inside the picture, so it works as long as the link or WiFi details stay the same.

Which colours scan best?

A dark pattern on a light background. Keep strong contrast between the two, and avoid light dots on a dark background because some phones cannot read it.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload it under "Make it yours". Qrafted raises the error correction automatically so the code still scans. Keep the logo small and test it with your phone.
